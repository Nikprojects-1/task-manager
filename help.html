Frequently Asked Questions

How do I create a new task?
To create a new task, click the "Add New Task" button on any page, fill in the task details including title, description, priority, and due date, then click "Save Task". You can also quickly add tasks from the dashboard using the Quick Add button.
Can I collaborate with team members?
Yes! TaskFlow supports team collaboration. You can invite team members from the Team page, assign tasks to specific members, share projects, and track team progress through the analytics dashboard.
How do I export my data?
You can export your data from the Settings page under "Data & Storage". Click the "Export" button to download all your tasks, projects, and settings as a JSON file. You can also export from your Profile page.
Is my data secure?
Absolutely! Your data is stored locally in your browser and is never transmitted to external servers without your explicit consent. We use industry-standard encryption for any data transmission.
Can I customize the appearance?
Yes! You can customize the theme, colors, and layout from the Settings page under "Appearance". Choose from light, dark, or auto themes, and enable compact mode if preferred.
